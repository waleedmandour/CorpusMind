City Council Approves Riverfront Park

The city council approved the new riverfront park project on Tuesday by a vote of 7 to 2. The project will cost 3.4 million dollars and is expected to open in 2028. Residents along the river have asked for more green space since 2019.

Council member Dana Reyes said the park will include two playgrounds, a cycling lane, and a small open-air theatre. Critics said the budget is too high. The mayor replied that the park will pay for itself through tourism.

Construction begins in March. The council will review progress every six months.
